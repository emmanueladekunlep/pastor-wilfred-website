About Pastor Wilfred

Passionate About Liberating Souls

Pastor Wilfred Aigbomobe is a passionate evangelist, international dynamic preacher, author, charismatic leader, and UK certified life coach. He is the founder and host of the Global Revival Blast podcast.

His divine mandate is to liberate souls from bondage and help people fulfill their destiny. His ministrations are characterized by the presence and raw power of the living God.
