Biography

Called to Liberate Souls

Wilfred Abumen Aigbomobe is a passionate evangelist, international dynamic preacher, author, charismatic leader, and UK certified life coach. He is the founder and host of the Global Revival Blast podcast, where he shares inspiring messages aimed at igniting spiritual revival and transformation worldwide.

He has a divine mandate from God and anointing to liberate souls from bondage and help people fulfill their destiny. His life revolves in the work of God and he is an anointed pastor, passionate about prayer, destiny fulfillment, revival and evangelism.

God accompanies his ministrations with great testimonies. Pastor Wilfred's ministrations are characterized by the presence and raw power of the living God that had touched so many souls in Europe and other parts of the world.
